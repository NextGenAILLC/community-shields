Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Community Shields is a civic unity project, now forming in Wisconsin, operated by NextGen AI. This policy explains what happens to the information you share through the "Get on the list" interest form on communityshields.com.

Not for emergencies. This website is not monitored around the clock and is not an emergency or reporting service. If someone is in danger, call 911.

What we collect

When you submit the interest form, we receive only what you choose to enter:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your town or community
  • Your role (for example, resident, business owner, or responder)
  • An optional message about what your town needs
  • Your confirmation that we may contact you

Please do not include sensitive personal, medical, or operational public-safety information in the form.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your inquiry, keep you informed about Community Shields in your area, and understand which towns and needs to focus on first. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered through Formspree, a form-handling service that receives and stores submissions on our behalf and forwards them to our inbox. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may record standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type.

This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Your light or dark display preference is saved only in your own browser.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries while Community Shields is forming and for as long as needed to follow up with you. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we will remove it from our inbox and form records.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information you sent us, or ask us to stop contacting you. Each email we send will also tell you how to opt out.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@nextgenerationai360.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
